The Government of India has announced a comprehensive agricultural development initiative aimed at improving farmers' income, increasing crop productivity, and promoting sustainable farming practices across the country. The new initiative aims to address farmers' challenges by providing financial assistance, improving irrigation facilities, and expanding access to agricultural training programs. Agriculture remains a major source of livelihood for millions of Indian households, facing issues such as unpredictable rainfall, rising fertilizer costs, limited access to modern equipment, and fluctuating market prices.
